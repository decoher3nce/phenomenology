The twelve interactions

Each is a distinct way a field or photon stream can be changed by the target, and each has its own observable, its own retrievable quantities and its own governing limit. Selecting a column header in the grid shows the same text with the techniques that use it.

      Method

      How to read and extend this

      The grid is a generative device, not a taxonomy. A technique is a choice of source, one or more interactions, a viewing geometry and a receiver class; enumerating the product space and asking why each cell is empty produces the concept list. The receiver axis is where the field is thinnest: almost everything is incoherent intensity or coherent heterodyne, with SMOS and geodetic VLBI the only TRL 9 correlation receivers and Swarm's helium magnetometer the only TRL 9 quantum sensor in routine Earth service.

      The TRL column changes the reading of the concept list. Sorted by TRL, the top is atom-interferometer gradiometry (5), TIR polarimetry (5), and a cluster at 4 (Rydberg, vibrometry, chronometric geodesy, Brillouin, Rayleigh–Brillouin, Ku/Ka reflectometry); these are IRAD-shaped because the step to 6 is an airborne or hosted-payload demonstration, not new physics. The bottom (2) is where the cheapest verdicts live. C04 and C17 were the first to be tried: each moved from 2 to 3 on a photon budget (the scripts are in the repository under scripts/calc), and each now needs a field night rather than a calculation.

      Two things deserved scrutiny before anyone quoted a number, and both were checked against the literature and mission status on 2026-10-17. First, the verify flags: of eleven, eight were settled, mostly upward, as Biomass, PREFIRE and the DQ-1 lidar reached routine operations, and three remain (Biomass tomographic products due 2027, the FY-4M sounder design, and the first ACES clock-comparison paper). Second, the novelty claims: of thirteen rated high, one survived. Most of the rest turned out to be funded programs or published proposals, and one, opportunistic laser occultation, had already been observed once by accident on a 2006 comm link. Every prior-art field now names what the search found and when.
